# Development roadmap

Estimates are planning ranges for a solo developer and depend on experience and scope.

## Phase 0 — Foundation
- Repository, linting, CI, design tokens
- Auth, profile, roles, database migrations
- RLS policy tests and private storage

## Phase 1 — MVP
- Dashboard and module navigation
- Study Center: upload, summary, quiz
- BanglaDoc: extract/summary/download
- Basic CV builder
- Usage limits, errors, feedback, analytics

## Phase 2 — Productivity
- Career applications and CareerPath roadmap
- Personal finance tracker
- Business inventory and sales reports

## Phase 3 — Community and marketplace
- Service listings and booking requests
- Freelancer projects and proposals
- Community events and volunteer management
- Travel planner and destination directory

## Phase 4 — Monetization
- Plans and entitlements
- Payment gateway checkout and verified webhooks
- Refund, reconciliation, support workflows
- AI cost limits and operational dashboards

## Phase 5 — Production hardening
- Accessibility, performance and mobile testing
- Threat modeling and security review
- Backups, restore drills, monitoring and incident response
- Terms, privacy, retention, and support policies
